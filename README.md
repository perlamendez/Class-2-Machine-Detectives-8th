# Class 2 · Machine detectives (for students who were absent)

Hi! In this class we learned the difference between simple machines and complex machines, and we found out if our ride is simple or complex. Please open the presentation and complete these steps in your notebook before next class:

1. Question 2 (R2): How do the parts of a system work together to make it function? Write your first idea in "What I thought." Only write what you think now; do not look for the answer.

2. Seesaw detectives: look at the 3 balanced seesaws in the presentation. Multiply kg × m on each side of every seesaw. What rule do you discover?

3. Ride detective: search on the internet how your ride works and answer:
a) Is your ride a simple or a complex machine? Why?
b) Which simple machines does it have? (lever, wheel and axle, pulley, inclined plane, wedge, screw)
c) What moves it? A motor, gravity or a person?
d) What stops it safely?

4. Exit ticket: decide if a ramp and an elevator are simple or complex machines, and write why.
